Pouch Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts an event's roasted almonds with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the pouches the almonds take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost an almond, and the minutes the packing takes. It uses no named constants. The almonds, the pouch size, the weekly use, the pouch price and the packing speed are yours to decide and read off your own orders, shelves and invoices. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your almonds, your pouches or your venue and does not know whether the almonds will really stay fresh, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a pouch is worth its price or an almond is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

The pouches truth

Pouches: the almonds divided by the almonds a pouch, rounded up, with what the last pouch holds.

The weeks truth

Weeks: the almonds divided by the almonds used a week, with the almonds left for the next week.

The cost truth

Cost: the pouches times the pouch price, to the cent, and spread over the almonds.

The time truth

Time: the almonds divided by the almonds packed a minute, rounded up, in minutes and in hours and minutes.

Pouch Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
